# Brand Briefing — Vocalis AI

🌐 **[Português (Brasil)](BRAND_BRIEF.md)** | **[English](BRAND_BRIEF.en.md)**

This document establishes the vision, positioning, tone of voice, and official visual style guidelines for **Vocalis AI**.

---

## 1. Overview & Purpose

**Vocalis AI** is a real-time conversation and technical interview copilot. Designed to run discreetly over video calls (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams), the system transcribes meeting audio with low latency, detects targeted questions, and generates precise contextual AI suggestions.

### Brand Tagline
> *"Real-time voice intelligence for decisive moments."*

---

## 2. Brand Pillars & Positioning

| Pillar | Description |
|:---|:---|
| **Technical Precision** | Direct, assertive responses backed by real engineering context. |
| **Low Latency & Speed** | Instant processing via Whisper + AI so answers arrive before your turn to speak. |
| **Discretion & Elegance** | Sleek floating interface that never obstructs call controls. |
| **Privacy & Open Source** | **GPL-2.0-only** copyleft guarantee with on-device Chrome AI capability. |

---

## 3. Visual Identity & Color System

The visual aesthetic of **Vocalis AI** adopts **3D Glassmorphism** over a high-contrast dark environment (*Dark Mode*).

### Official Color Palette

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  Primary Dark (Background): #0f172a (Slate Dark 900)                  |
|  Royal Blue Glass (Ribbon): #3b82f6 (Blue 500)                        |
|  Electric Violet (AI Spark):#8b5cf6 (Violet 500)                      |
|  Cyber Cyan (Highlight):    #06b6d4 (Cyan 500)                        |
|  VAD Active (Active Speech):#22c55e (Emerald 500)                     |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
```
